How the Sportsbook projection is calculated

It is the betting market's own forecast, converted with your league's scoring. Nothing about the house model is involved, which is what makes it a genuinely independent second opinion.

  1. Pull this week's stored player props from every book (currently DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM).
  2. Take one line per book per market — the Over row's number. The Under carries the same line, so counting both would double a book's vote.
  3. Take the median across books. Median, not mean, so a single book posting an outlier line cannot drag the number.
  4. Multiply that expected stat by your scoring weight and sum the categories.

Read it with these in mind

Only six markets are converted. There is no interception, fumble, or two-point market, so nothing here can ever subtract points. Every QB number is optimistic by construction{% if sportsbook.example and sportsbook.example.is_qb %} — the {{ sportsbook.example.name }} total above carries no deduction for interception risk at all{% endif %}.

A TD line is a threshold, not an expected count. Books post rushing TDs as "over 0.5", so this treats that as half a touchdown for everyone who has the market — a goal-line back and a long-shot both land on 0.5 × 6 = 3.0 points.

The line is used raw. No vig is removed and the attached odds are ignored, so this is the market's midpoint rather than a true probability-weighted mean.

Kickers and defenses get nothing. No market maps to them, so they show as — and fall back to the other sources.
